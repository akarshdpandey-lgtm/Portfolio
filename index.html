Deep Learning-Based Tuberculosis Screening from Chest X-Ray Images Using DenseNet-121

Author: Akarsh Pandey — Dept. of ECE, IPS Academy

A DenseNet-121 classifier for TB vs No-TB chest X-rays on the TBX11K Simplified dataset — 8,399 unique images (7,600 No-TB, 799 TB), trained on 6,599 and validated on 1,800 with class-weighted loss and ROC-AUC-based model selection.

98.94% AccuracyROC-AUC 0.998996.00% Sensitivity99.31% SpecificityF1 95.29%

Experimental screening-support prototype — not a diagnostic tool. No independent test set or clinical validation yet.

Smart Traffic • Edge Vision • Sole Author Will be Published Soon

DART-Signal: Decision-Aware, Self-Monitoring, Power-Adaptive Edge-Vision Framework for Traffic Signal Control

Author: Akarsh Pandey — Dept. of ECE, IPS Academy

A closed-loop framework for a four-way intersection combining power-adaptive vision (Low / Medium / High modes), a camera-health monitor (Qcam), uncertainty-triggered verification, and a risk-constrained offline-RL controller wrapped in deterministic safety shields — with multimodal emergency-vehicle priority and graceful camera-failure fallback. Evaluated via a SUMO + ESP32 prototype protocol.

YOLOv8 + ByteTrackOffline RL + ShieldsSUMO • ESP32

Design targets: 74.5% lower mean delay vs fixed-time and 48.6% lower mean edge power vs static 30 FPS, with zero shield violations under degradation — pending confirmation by final measured runs.

Power Systems • Numerical Methods • Co-Author

Diagnosis and Remediation of Newton-Raphson Load Flow Divergence in the IEEE 300-Bus Power System

Mohd. Firoz, Dr. Manish Sahajwani, Akarsh Pandey, Gourav Singh Kushwah, Vijendra Solanki & Aniket Sharma — IPS Academy

Diagnoses NR divergence on the IEEE 300-bus system (300 buses, 333 branches, 58 generators) — ill-conditioned Jacobian, PV-to-PQ switching, step overshoot and sparse LU instability — and fixes it with warm-start initialization, Levenberg–Marquardt regularization, Armijo step control and reactive-power hysteresis.
